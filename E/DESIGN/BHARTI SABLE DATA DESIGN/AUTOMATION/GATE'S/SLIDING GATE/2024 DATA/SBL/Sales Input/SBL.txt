Providing  motorised M.S. sliding gate of approved design and drawing As Per European Standard EN 13241-1:2003+A1.2011. Clear opening dimension shall be 10m and height of  gate shall be 2.4m, sliding action shall be unidirectional along  one side  and on  the other side manual swing  wicket  gate  of  approved  design  and  drawing  of  dimension  1mx2.2m  shall  be  provided.Gate  frame  shall  be  of  MS  tubular  section 100x100x3.2mm and internal members shall be MS tubular sections 40x40x2mm or nearest available size, bottom rail shall be 20mm dia bar welded  over  ISMB  100,  heavy  duty  bottom  rollers  with  dual  bearing  mechanism  shall  be  provided  for  free  sliding  movement  of  gate.All  MS members  shall be  painted   with  enamel paint  of  approved  make and  shade  over primer coat.   The movement  of  gate  shall be  controlled by means of  push button arrangement. The rate shall include cost of  horizontal bottom rails,rollers, lead and lift of  all materials required,motors, supports required, push button arrangement etc all complete as directed by Engineer in charge.
